# Privacy, in plain language.

Effective date: 11 October 2026

For privacy questions or requests, contact [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}).

## The iPhone app

Swolseye does not collect or transmit your app data to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs or app-initiated network access.

The app stores your current match, completed turns, Undo history, player names, language and scoring-input preference locally on your iPhone. This storage lets you resume play after closing the app. Player names are names you choose; you do not need to enter real names.

Swolseye does not provide cloud synchronization. Normal iOS device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. Apple controls those services; see [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Managing local data

Starting **New Match** replaces the match and Undo history after confirmation. It retains player names and preferences. To remove all locally stored app data, use **Delete App** in iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Swolseye. Offloading the app retains its data. Manage device backups separately in Apple's settings.

There is no Swolseye account or server-side match record to delete. The developer cannot access or recover your locally stored match.

## This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, external fonts or embedded third-party content. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

## Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information or attachments you provide. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. Correspondence is handled through Outlook, provided by Microsoft; see [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

Please avoid sending sensitive information. You can ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing the address above. This does not delete copies held in your own email account or independently managed provider backups.

## Changes and questions

We will update this page if these practices change, with the effective date above. Contact us with questions or requests concerning information you have sent us.
