# Let's sort it out.

Questions, bugs, feedback or privacy requests: email the developer of Swolseye.
{: .lead}

[{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }})

## Reporting a problem?

Please include:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version.
- Your Swolseye version, shown in the app's Settings.
- What you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
- A screenshot if useful, with personal information removed.

Never send passwords or other sensitive information. We don't need access to your Apple Account to help.

## Before you write

The [support guide]({{ '/support/' | relative_url }}) covers getting started and save errors. The [FAQ]({{ '/faq/' | relative_url }}) explains scoring, Undo and local data.

When you email us, your address and the information you choose to send are used to handle your request. See our [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
