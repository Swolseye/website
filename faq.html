The short answers.

A few things to know before your next match.

Which games can I play?

Swolseye supports one two-player match of 501, with a double or inner bull required to finish. It doesn't offer other game types, extra players or a match archive.

How do I enter doubles and triples?

Tap the number repeatedly: triple 20 is three taps on 20. There is no multiplier control. Taps represent points, not a physical dart count, and a turn cannot exceed 180 points. Tap End Turn when your turn is over.

What do checkout suggestions mean?

They suggest a route from your current score using up to three physical darts. S means single, D means double, T means triple, and Bull is the inner bull worth 50. They don't track how many darts you have left in your turn. Some scores have no checkout route.

Why does the app ask me to confirm a finish?

Point entry can't tell whether the last physical dart hit a double. When you reach zero, confirm a double or inner bull to finish. Otherwise the turn is a bust.

Can I undo a bust or a finished match?

Yes. Undo works across turn boundaries and can reopen a finished match. The final scoring input and finish confirmation are undone together. Undo survives closing the app, but New Match replaces the old match and its Undo history.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app has no accounts or network features. Match data stays in local app storage. Your iPhone's normal backup settings may still apply; that is different from app-provided cloud sync.

Can I change the controls or language?

In Settings, choose Dartboard or Number Grid. Number Grid offers larger rectangular targets. You can also choose English, Nederlands, or System Default. Changing these preferences doesn't reset the match.

Can I see previous matches?

You can see completed turns in the current match using Show All. Starting a new match permanently replaces that match; there is no archive of previous matches.

How do I delete my data?

New Match replaces the current game and its Undo history but keeps names and preferences. To remove all local app data, delete Swolseye using iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Swolseye → Delete App. Offloading is different and retains app data. Backups are managed separately through Apple's settings.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no registration or account to delete. See the privacy policy for app storage, website hosting and support email details.
