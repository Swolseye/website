# A little help. Then back to darts.

Swolseye keeps score for one two-player game of 501 on your iPhone. Start here if you have a scoring question or something isn't working.
{: .lead}

## Get started

1. Tap a player's name to change it. Player 1 starts; both players begin on 501.
2. Enter points with the dartboard or number grid. To enter 60, tap 20 three times. Each tap is a scoring input, not necessarily one physical dart.
3. Tap **End Turn** to switch players. A turn can total up to 180 points.
4. Reach exactly zero and confirm that your last physical dart was a double or inner bull to win.

## Correct a score

Use **Undo** to reverse the latest action, including an ended turn or a bust. You can keep undoing earlier actions. There is no Redo, so enter the correct points to continue.

## A score went back up

That's normally a bust. Going below zero or leaving exactly one resets your score to the start of the turn and switches players. Reaching zero without a double or inner bull is also a bust.

## Your match and saved data

Your current match, completed turns, Undo history, player names and preferences are saved on your iPhone. Closing and reopening the app restores the saved state. There is no account, online match archive or app-provided cloud sync.

**New Match** replaces the current match and its Undo history after confirmation. Player names and preferences stay. Use this only when you're ready to start again.

## If saving or opening a match fails

Use the app's **Retry** option first. Check that your iPhone has available storage. If a save fails, changes pause to protect the last saved state. Closing the app before a retry succeeds can lose the unsaved action.

If the problem continues, contact us before deleting or reinstalling the app: removing the app can remove your locally stored match. We cannot recover match data from a server because Swolseye doesn't send it to one.

## Still need a hand?

[Create a support issue on GitHub](https://github.com/swolseye/website/issues/new?template=support.md) to ask a question or report a problem. You can also [browse existing issues](https://github.com/swolseye/website/issues) to see whether it has already been reported. A GitHub account is required to post.

GitHub issues are public. For private questions or personal information, use email instead.

[Email Swolseye support](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}) at **{{ site.support_email }}**. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version from Settings, and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots can help; remove anything you don't want to share.
